# Cook White Rice in an Instant Pot Duo 3 QT

**Status:** Draft | **Last updated:** September 28, 2026 |
**Applies to:** [Instant Pot Duo Multi-Cooker 3 QT]


Use this guide to prepare white rice using the Rice program on the Instant Pot Multi-Cooker 3 Qt. 


## Before you begin

1. Place the instant pot on a clean, safe, and level counter top in the kitchen.
2. Ensure the instant pot is away from the stove and other heat sources.
3. Keep the pot unplugged while cleaning and preparing its components.
4. Read the safety instructions in the manufacturer's manual.

## Ingredients and Equipment

- Measure and add approximately 20 oz (567g) of SEKKA extra fancy premium rice. 
- Cold drinking water - 20 oz (591 ml), or until it reaches about a fingernail's height above the dry rice level

## Prepare the rice

- Remove the lid and lift out the inner pot
If necessary, give it a quick scrub and rinse thoroughly with warm water and soap. should take about 15-20 seconds.
- Measure the dry rice and place it in the inner pot
- Rinse the rice thoroughly under cold tap water until most of the impurities are gone and the water turns near transparent.
- Dry the bottom of the inner pot, and ensure it is free of debris and moisture.
- Place the inner pot back into cooker base


The instant pot will beep briefly, and the LED panel will display "ON" in red.
- Turn the outer lid counter-clockwise until it hits a physical stop, or when you can't turn it anymore. Usually at this point, the lid's downwards arrow will have aligned with the unlock symbol marking on the edge of the outer lid
- Lift the outer lid up, and inside will be the inner metal pot and cooking base. at this time, the LED display should go from ON > LID


## Start cooking

1. After placing the inner metal pot back into the instant pot, secure the outer lid.
2. Align it with the unlock position, and then turn it clockwise until it stops. This is the lock position, and the LED display change go from LID to ON. 
3. After securing the lid, ensure the pressure exhaust near the back of the lid is positioned to the 10 o'clock position (Seal).
4. Connect the power cord securely and plug the cooker directly into a suitable wall outlet.
5. Press the **rice** button. Refer to the table below to determine appropriate setting. 

| Setting | Less | Normal | High |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Cooking Time | 8 minutes | 12 minutes | 15 minutes |
| Best for | Al-dente/ white rice | normal texture/white rice | softer texture/white rice |

5. Turn "keep warm" off if you plan to serve the rice right after pressure release.
6. Allow the **rice** preset program to run.

>Tip: Instant Pot Duo is able to automatically adjust cooking time based on volume of the rice. The LED will display "auto". 

The total preparation time includes pressurization, cooking, and pressure release.


## Release pressure and serve

1. When cooking finishes, the instant pot produces 4-5 long beeps and the LED panel will display "end".
2. Leave the steam-release handle in **Sealing** and wait 10 minutes.
3. Following the model's instructions, move the handle to **Venting** to release any remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the escaping steam.
4. Wait until pressure is fully released and the float valve drops.
5. Turn the lid to the unlocked position and lift it away from your face.
6. Fluff the rice with a rice paddle and serve.

Alternatively, allow the cooker to depressurize fully through natural release. The time required varies.

> [!WARNING]
> Never cover either valve with a towel or other object.
> Never force the lid open. Use oven mitts when handling the hot inner po

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The lid will not open | Do not apply force. Wait until pressure is fully released and the float valve towards the back drops. |
| The cooker displays a warning or error | Stop and consult the troubleshooting section of the model's manual. |
| The cooked rice is too firm or too soft | Record the quantities and setting used. Adjust and retest the recipe before revising the published instructions. |
