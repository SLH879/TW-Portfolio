# Multi-Platform Survey Administration and Data Review: Research Support Case Study
**Document ID:** Case-surveyadministration-datareview-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** August 2026 | **Audience:** Academics | **Published Date:** Draft

## Overview

This case study documents survey and research work I previously completed for an independent graduate-level research project in late 2024. The research examined how social media use impacted consumer behaviour, identity formation, and mental well-being among Chinese young adults between ages 18 to 24. My role in the project included developing and deploying an online questionnaire, translating the questionnaire between English and Mandarin, administering it across multiple survey platforms, supporting participant recruitment and sampling, consolidating the results, and analyzing the collected data for trends, recurring, and abnormal findings. To protect the project and participants, this portfolio version does not reproduce the original questionnaire, dissertation topic, institution, client, and response data. Any examples included herein are recreated and fictionalized.


## Project Context and Responsibilities

The project used a qualitative review design structured around 17 comprehensive close and open ended online survey questions.

The target population was Chinese young adults between ages 18 to 24 who used social media.

The survey explored many broad areas:
- social media usage
- pressures to conform
- identity and self presentation
- impacts on personal well-being

The final survey was designed on SurveyMonkey; subsequently deployed and administered on:
- Google Forms
- WJX.cn
