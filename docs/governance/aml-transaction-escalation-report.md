## 1. Purpose

This document outlines a suspicious transaction monitoring scenario within the Canadian casino and gaming sector involving potential structuring activity, layering indicators, and escalation considerations under FINTRAC reporting requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This assessment applies to transaction monitoring, customer activity review, and suspicious transaction escalation procedures within casino environments regulated under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA).

## 4. Baseline Customer Behaviour

Under normal circumstances, the customer visited the casino approximately twice per month and typically purchased approximately $2,000 CAD in chips using debit transactions or smaller cash payments.

Historical activity primarily involved blackjack and slot play over extended periods, followed by chip redemption values generally consistent with original buy-in amounts. Prior transaction history aligned with expected recreational gambling behaviour.

## 6. Suspicious Activity Indicators

The following indicators contributed to escalation concerns:

- Transaction behaviour inconsistent with past customer patterns and stated occupation
- Repeated cash transactions conducted immediately below the $10,000 CAD reporting threshold
- Consecutive high-value cash buy-ins over multiple days
- Predominant use of small denomination currency
- Minimal gaming activity relative to chip purchase volume
- Redemption request through bank draft payout

When viewed collectively, these indicators suggest potential structuring behaviour intended to avoid reporting thresholds, followed by possible layering activity through conversion of cash into negotiable financial instruments.

---

## 7. Escalation Assessment

Based on cumulative transaction review, customer behaviour analysis, and observed reporting threshold avoidance patterns, there are reasonable grounds to suspect potential money laundering activity.

Particular concern was raised by:

- Repeated sub-threshold cash transactions
- Limited gaming activity relative to cash volume
- Request for bank draft redemption
- Activity inconsistent with historical patron behaviour and expected income profile

Observed activity patterns support escalation for suspicious transaction review under FINTRAC reporting guidance.

---

## 8. Actions Taken

- Customer activity flagged for enhanced AML review
- Internal escalation initiated to compliance personnel
- Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) prepared for FINTRAC submission
- Monitoring recommendation issued for repeated sub-threshold cash activity patterns

---

## 9. Monitoring Recommendations

- Enhance cumulative cash threshold monitoring across multi-day activity windows
- Reinforce employee awareness training regarding structuring indicators
- Review escalation procedures involving bank draft redemption requests
- Continue periodic review of recurring patron cash activity patterns
