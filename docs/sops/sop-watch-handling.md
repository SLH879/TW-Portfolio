# User Guide: Automatic Mechanical Watch Operation
**Document ID:** SOP-WATCH-001 | **Version:** 1.2 | **Initial Date:** February 2026 | **Last Updated:** May 2026 | **Status:** Published

---

## 1. Purpose
This guide provides standardized procedures for the initial setup, daily operation, and long-term maintenance of automatic mechanical watches. 

## 2. Scope
This guide covers the operations of automatic and mechanical wrist watches with manual winding calendar (day and day) functions. Variations may exist between different make and movement configuration.

## 3. Entry Criteria
This procedure may be initiated when:
- The watch stops operating after the depletion of its power reserve
- The watch requires manual time and date setting after prolonged period of non-usage
- The end-user wishes to manually adjust the watch to correct the current time and date

## 4. Inputs
- Automatic mechanical watch in stopped/depleted state
- Correct date/time reference available
- User access to crown and adjustment functions

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Crown Release
1. If equipped with a screw-down crown, rotate the crown counter-clockwise until released into the operational position.

### 5.2 Manual Winding
1. Rotate the crown clockwise approximately 30–50 turns to restore mainspring tension.
2. Stop winding once resistance stabilizes.

> **Note:** Automatic movements typically include a slipping clutch to reduce over-winding risk.

## 6. Exit Criteria
This procedure is considered complete when:
- The date and time have been proeprly set
- The watch has resumed stable operation (seconds hand sweeps clockwise)
- The crown is screwed back to its original position (position 0 for those with screwdown crowns and position 1 for those without)

## 7, Outputs
- Watch synchronized to correct date and time
- Mainspring sufficiently wound
- Crown returned to secured operating position
- Watch restored to normal operation


## 8. Safety and Cautionary Notices

> [!WARNING]
> **Date Setting Restriction:** Avoid adjusting the date mechanism between **9:00 PM and 3:00 AM; 10PM and 2AM; or 11PM and 1PM** (depending on the make and model of the watch).    
> Engaging the date-flip gear during this window can cause permanent mechanical failure of the calendar (day/date) wheel.  

> [!CAUTION]
> **Water Resistance:** Ensure the crown is fully depressed or screwed down before exposure to water. Failure to secure the crown voids all water resistance ratings and may lead to internal oxidation.  
> **Fully Stopped:** Ensure that the Watch is fully stopped for watches with hackable movements. This does not apply to watches with non-hackable movements (seconds hand will continue moving).  
> **Dry Hands:** Dry hands are recommended whenever handling a watch with the crown screwed out to reduce risks of moisture getting into the watch.  
> **Flat Surface:** Flat surface recommended for adjustment to minimize risks of physical damage (e.g, scratches, dents, scuffs).  

## 9. Initial Setup Procedure (From "Dead" State)
Follow these steps to re-engage the mainspring and synchronize time after a period of non-wear:
1. **Unsecure the Crown:** If equipped with a screw-down crown, rotate it counter-clockwise until it "pops" out from **Position 0 (innermost position)** into **Position 2 (outermost position)**.  
2. **Manual Wind:** Rotate the crown clockwise 30–50 full turns (this depends on mainspring tension).   
   * *Note: Automatic movements feature a sliding clutch to prevent over-winding; this is largely absent in mechanical hand-wound watches.*  
3. **Set Reference Time:** Pull the crown to its outermost position (**Position 2**). Rotate the hands forward until the date changes to identify the midnight transition point. For many watches without instant date change capability, the date flip wheel will remain in a partially engaged state between approximately 11pm to 1am. To ensure a safe window for date adjustment, set the time to **6:00 AM**.  
4. **Set Date:** Push the crown to **Position 1**. Rotate the crown (usually clockwise for date, and counter-clockwise for day of the week) to the current numerical date.  
5. **Final Secure:** Push the crown back to **Position 0**. If applicable, screw the crown down firmly to ensure a water-tight seal.

## 11. Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Potential Cause | Corrective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Watch stops overnight | Low power reserve | Increase daily wear to 8+ hours, gently shake the watch for ~30 seconds, or manual wind. |
| Significant time gain | Magnetization | Use a demagnetizing/degaussing tool or visit a watchmaker. |
| Date fails to flip | Improper setup of date | Repeat Step 3 of the Setup Procedure. |
| Watch runs slow | Possible magnetization or lubrication issue | Degauss, or visit a certified watchmaker. |
