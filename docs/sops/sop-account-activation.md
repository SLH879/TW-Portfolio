# Employee Account Activation Guide 
**Document ID:** SOP-Account-Activation-001 | **Version:** 1.1 | **Last Updated:** June 2026 | **Audience:** Employees | **Published Date:** June 2026


# Purpose
This guide illustrates the creation, validation, and access assignment of internal employee accounts. Employees are eligible for account creation after they have been onboarded

# Scope
The guide applies to all employees within the company. It encompasses:
- Account creation
- Account initial setup
- Access assignment
- Permission provisioning

# Prerequisites
Before an employee can be provisioned a company account, they must have first met the following:
- Attended and completed onboarding sessions
> **Note:** To be given a temporary username and passcode
- Employment status confirmed
- Eligible for receiving temporary login credentials
- Corporate email assigned
- Manager assigned

# Inputs
- Temporary username and password provided by the manager after onboarding
- User defined username and password changed after initial login
- Approved multi-factor authentication (MFA) such as Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator

# Procedure
## Activating through onboarding
1. Upon accepting the offer and formally joining the company, the employee will be required to join the onboarding process 
2. After logged completion of the process, the employee will be provisioned access to their corporate (employee) emails
3. Within the email will be an activation invitation mail to the account portal, prompting activation within 24 hours
4. The activation link will bring employee to the employee account portal, instructing the creation of an account with the provided temporary username and password
5. Upon completion, a formal log will be created in the company database signifying success in account creation 
6. The IT system administrator (helpdesk) will help validate the account, grant access, and assign privileges and permission to the user
7. A confirmation will be sent to the employee informing finalization and granted access
8. After receiving the email, employee will be urged to set up MFA (Google, Microsoft Authenticator) within 24 hours

## Activating through IT helpdesk
1. If an employee does not gain access to their corporate emails or does not receive the invitation email with a working link within the first 24 hours, they can contact the systems administrator (IT) for support
2. The systems administrator can troubleshoot with manual account creations, and for new emails and activation links to be generated and sent to employees
> **Note**: IT systems administrators can take up to one business day to respond and troubleshoot

# Outputs
- Created and accessible corporate email
- Employee account that can be accessed 
- Approved MFA has been set up and integrated into account

# Success Criteria
- Employee has access to their corporate email
- Employee has access to their corporate account (completed during onboarding or systems administrator assistance)
- Employee has finished MFA setup
- Access consistent across company and personal devices

# Troubleshooting
| Potential Problems | Solutions |
| --- | ---- | 
| No access to corporate email | Reach out to IT for manual creation or access override |
| No invitation email | Reach out to manager or IT for new invitation to be sent |
| Unable to login | Reach out to IT for access and credentials support |
| Unable to setup MFA | Use the phone text/call alternative, or reach out to IT for assistance |
| Don't know how to contact IT | Email: Internal IT Helpdesk, Phone: Internal Helpdesk Extension |
