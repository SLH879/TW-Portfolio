# Account Provisioning Guide 
**Document ID:** SOP-Account-Provision | **Version:** 1.0 | **Initial Date:** June 2026 | **Published Date:** June 2026


# Purpose
This guide illustrates the creation, validation, and access assignment of internal employee accounts. Employees are eligible for account creation after they have been onboarded

# Scope
The guide applies to all employees within the company. It encompasses:
- Account creation
- Account initial setup
- Access assignment
- Permission provisoning

# Prerequisities
Before an employee can be provisioned a company account, they must have first met the following:
- Attended and completed onboarding sessions
> **Note:** To be given a temporary username and passcode
- Employment status confirmed
- Eligible for receiving temporary login credentials
- Corporate email assigned
- Manager assigned

# Inputs
- Temporary username and password provided by the manager after onboarding
- User defined username and password changed after initial login
- Approved multi-factor authentication (MFA) such as Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator

# Procedure
## Activating through onboarding
1. Upon joining the company, employee will be required to join the onboarding process 
2. After completion, employee will be provided access to their employee emails
3. Within the email will be an activation invitation email to the account portal, prompting activation
4. The activation link will bring employee to the employee account portal, prompting the creation of an account with the provided temporary username and password
5. Upon completion, both the manager and the IT team will receive an automated notification
6. IT helpdesk will help validate the account, grant access, and assign privileges and permission to the user
7. A confirmation will be sent to the employee informing finalization and granted access
8. After receiving the email, employee will be prompted to set up MFA (Google, Microsoft Authenticator)

## Activating through helpdesk
1. If an employee does not have access to their emails or does not receive the invitation email with a working link, they can contact the helpdesk for support
2. IT department (helpdesk) can help with manual account creations and for new emails and links to be generated and sent to employees
> **Note**: Depending on the availability of helpdesk staff, this can expedite or slow down account creation

# Outputs
- Created and accessible employee email
- Employee account that can be accessed 
- Approved MFA has been set up and integrated into account

# Success Criteria
- EMmployee has access to their employee email
- Employee has access to their employee account (achieved through onboarding or helpdesk assistance)
- Employee has approved MFA setup
- Access consistent across company and personal devices

# Troubleshooting
| Potential Problems | Solutions |
| --- | ---- | 
| No access to employee email | Reach out to IT for manual creation or access override |
| No inivitation email | Reach out to manager or IT for new invitation to be sent |
| Unable to login | Reach out to IT for access and credentials support |
| Unable to setup MFA | Use the phone text/call alternative, or reach out to IT for assistance |
