## Subject

Scheduled Online Banking Maintenance Notice

---

## Notification Copy

To support ongoing system performance and security improvements, scheduled maintenance will be performed on select online banking services during the following maintenance window:

- **Date:** Saturday, May 16, 2026  
- **Time:** 1:00 AM – 5:00 AM EST  

During this period, members may experience intermittent access issues or temporary unavailability affecting:

- Online banking access  
- Mobile banking services  
- External transfer requests  
- Account notification updates  

Debit card transactions, ATM access, and scheduled recurring payments are expected to remain operational during the maintenance window.

We recommend completing any time-sensitive transactions prior to the scheduled maintenance period or leaving them until after the maintenance is complete.

> **Important:** Maintenance notifications will only be communicated through official company channels. Members should avoid responding to unsolicited emails or messages requesting account credentials or verification codes.

We appreciate your patience as these updates are completed to improve system reliability and account security.

For additional assistance, please contact Member Services through the official support channels.
