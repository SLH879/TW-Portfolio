## Subject

Action Recommended: Review Your Account Security Settings

---

## Notification Copy

We recently updated several account security features to improve protection for member accounts and online access.

As part of these updates, members are encouraged to review and confirm their current multi-factor authentication (MFA) settings and account recovery information.

To review your settings:

1. Sign in to online banking
2. Navigate to “Security Settings”
3. Confirm your phone number and recovery email
4. Verify that MFA is enabled on your account

Members who do not complete verification may experience temporary restrictions on certain account features until account information is confirmed.

> **Important:** **Company name** will never request passwords, verification codes, or full account credentials through email or text message.

If you require assistance, please contact Member Services through the official support portal or customer support line.
